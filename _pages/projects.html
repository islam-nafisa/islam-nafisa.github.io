Undergraduate Projects

1) Plant Disease Detection System using Convolution Neural Network

The agricultural output of the nation is impacted by pest-infected plants and crops. In order to find and detect diseases, farmers or other experts typically closely monitor the plants. But this process is often expensive, time-consuming, and inaccurate. Finding a spot on the leaves of the infected plant is one way to identify plant diseases. This work aims to develop a disease recognition model that is backed by the classification of leaf images. We are using a convolution neural network (CNN) in image processing to identify plant illnesses. One type of artificial neural network designed especially to analyze pixel input and be utilized in image recognition is the convolutional neural network (CNN).

3) Design and Analysis of 1-Bit SRAM Cell using Cadence Virtuos, VLSI

Static Random-Access Memory (SRAM) cells, which act as quick and effective memory storage components, are crucial components of contemporary digital integrated circuits. In this report, 1-Bit SRAM cell is implemented and its schematic, simulation results, truth tables, and stick diagram is analyzed. The performance characteristics of the SRAM cell, including delay and power consumption, are carefully analyzed using comprehensive simulations using cadence virtuoso. The outcomes show that the 1-bit SRAM cell provides competitive performance traits while taking up only a little amount of space.

4) Dielectric Resonator Antenna for WLAN Applications

A simple Dielectric Resonator Antenna (DRA) for WLANapplication is proposed in this paper. The frequency range to operate WLAN application is 2GHz to 10 GHz. The DRA material used here is Ceramic which is high directive material with relative permittivity 30. Here DR is fed by a modified 50 ohm microstrip line situated on top of the substrate. The simulated and measured impedance bandwidth achieved at 2.9767 GHZ is 11.8% (2.8-3.1534); whilst for 7 GHZ at 20% (6.3-7.7) for S11 is less than-10 dB. And the gain of the proposed antenna is 4.90 dBi and 2.42 dBi at 2.16 GHz and 10GHZ respectively. Results are simulated using Ansoft High-frequency structural simulator (HFSS) for the study of impedance bandwidth, return loss, radiation pattern, and antenna gain. Furthermore, the antenna has been fabricated and tested. The measured characteristics of the proposed antenna are in good agreement with the simulated results.

6) Digital Water Level Detector Using PIC Microcontroller

This work involves the design and implementation of a digital water level detector using a PIC 16F877A microcontroller. PIC 16F877A is frequently used in the industry due to its convenience and FLASH memory technology which enables it to write-erase as many times as possible. Three open wire ends were placed at the container and each time water would short one of the terminals the microcontroller would yield an output based on the code burnt to it written in C. A 16x2 LCD display was used to show the output from the microcontroller. An additional piezo-buzzer was connected with the highest terminal to warn when the container gets filled. The project performed as expected being able to detect 4 levels of water and warning via buzzer when the tank gets filled. Keywords—PIC microcontroller, Piezo-buzzer, LCD, water level, detect.
